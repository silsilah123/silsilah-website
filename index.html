Tips for parents

the camel rope method

why children forget

The Prophet ﷺ compared the Qur'an to a tethered camel: if its owner keeps hold of the rope, he keeps it, and if he lets go, it wanders away (Sahih al Bukhari and Muslim).

Little and often keeps a surah firmly tied. Our teachers build short daily revision into every child's routine, so what they memorise stays with them.
